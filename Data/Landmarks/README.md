# Blender landmark models: Basilica del Santo and Prato della Valle

Two procedural Blender models in the game's graphic style: the game's own Padova materials, metric tiling and modelled (not photographed) ornament. Every position, footprint, eave and water level comes from the municipal survey. Anything above an eave, and all ornament, is **reference-informed typology**, not a measurement.

## Rebuild

```sh
.context/geo-venv/bin/python Tools/landmarks/extract_sources.py        # survey + OSM -> Data/Landmarks/sources.json
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup \
    --python Tools/blender/build_landmarks.py -- santo prato --render    # .blend, .fbx, manifest, previews
unity command run_script --file AgentScripts/BuildLandmarks.cs --entry BuildLandmarks.Build
unity command run_tests --mode editor                                   # LandmarkModelTests
python3 Tools/verify_world.py --build --measure                         # includes the santo and prato cases
```

Blender runs as its own headless process, not through the shared Blender MCP session, so it cannot interfere with other agents' Blender work. `extract_sources.py` needs the DBT archive in `.context/geodata/padova-dbt/` and the OSM snapshot `.context/landmarks/prato-osm.json` (Overpass, `tourism=artwork` and `man_made=obelisk` nodes in 45.3965–45.4005 N, 11.8740–11.8790 E, base 2026-09-29T10:10Z). The committed `sources.json` lets a fresh clone rebuild the models without either download.

| File | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `sources.json` | Frozen evidence: DBT records in local metres, the OSM points and the WorldPlan ground model. Also records the SHA-256 hashes of the source shapefiles and WorldPlan. |
| `santo-build.json`, `prato-build.json` | Anchor, triangle counts, bounds, statue placement report |
| `Blender/Landmarks/*.blend` | Editable Blender sources, regenerated by the script |
| `Assets/Art/Landmarks/*.fbx` | Unity assets. `Landmarks.json` tells `WorldContext` which survey records the models now draw. |

In Unity the FBX is placed at `anchor` (east, up, north) and rotated +90° about X. `*_Collision` is a hidden MeshCollider on layer 9. `Prato_Ground` is walkable ground on layer 8, and `*_Water` has no collider.

## Basilica di Sant'Antonio

**Surveyed (DBT 2007, IODL 2.0).** The model reproduces the geometry of 30 `UN_VOL` records: nave, front block, ambulatory, chapels, aisles, seven drums plus the Treasury rotunda, two campanili, two turrets and seven projecting loggia bays. Footprints, drum holes, bases and eaves are exact. `LandmarkModelTests` checks the collision bounds against the survey to 2 cm, including the campanile eave at 56.1 m. `WorldContext` no longer draws these 30 records.

**Published.** Wikipedia it (2026) gives:
- campanili 68 m high, which sets the spire tips;
- façade about 28 m, which sets the gable apex;
- eight domes, with the conical dome over the crossing carrying an angel;
- five recessed portal arches;
- Gattamelata base 7.8 × 4.1 m and statue 3.4 × 3.9 m.

**Typology.** The following were drawn from Wikimedia Commons photographs used only as drawing references (`.context/landmarks-ref/`):
- dome profiles (0.92 rise ratio, 32 standing seams);
- cone height (14 m), lantern and spire proportions;
- archetti pensili, windows, pilaster strips, the loggia arcade and balustrades, rose window, biforas and doors.

No photograph is textured onto the model.

**Known conflicts, kept visible:**
- **Body roof.** The DBT eave of the main body (29.2 m) is almost the same as the drum eaves (28.3–30.1 m). The model keeps it, so the domes rise from a nearly flat lead roof instead of from the pitched roofs seen in photographs.
- **Façade gable.** The front block's DBT eave is 25.17 m, but the published façade height is about 28 m. The gable therefore rises only from 25.17 to 28 m, which makes it flatter than the real one.
- **Gattamelata.** `MN_UVOL` records a height of 18.7 m, while the published dimensions total about 11 m. The model uses the DBT pedestal footprint with the published proportions. The horse's heading is approximate.

## Prato della Valle

**Surveyed (DBT 2007):**
- **Canal:** the union of eight `AB_CDA` polygons. The water surface is at the median surveyed water-edge elevation, −3.695 m local, about 0.7 m below the fitted ground.
- **Bridges:** four `PONTE` outlines. The crown heights come from their surveyed deck elevations.
- **Fountain:** the `MN_EDI_NOVOL:2951` basin outline.

`WorldContext` no longer draws those canal patches or the 25 m ground cells in `ownGround`. The model supplies that ground, cut around the canal.

**Statue numbering.** There are 88 positions, following the Commons plan *Prato della Valle-Pianta Statue.jpg*:
- 78 statues;
- obelisks at 11, 12, 33, 34, 55, 56, 77 and 78;
- empty pedestals at 45 and 88.

This matches Wikipedia it.

**Statue positions.** Positions come from OpenStreetMap artwork nodes (ODbL), snapped onto the canal edge (mean 1.06 m, max 2.67 m). Three kinds of position are filled differently:
- **Missing from OSM:** refs 45, 49, 50 and 88 are interpolated.
- **OSM outlier:** ref 30's node lies 13.6 m from the canal, so its position is interpolated too.
- **Obelisk and empty positions:** obelisks and empty pedestals use the same placement, whatever their OSM tags.

**Typology.**
- Figures are generic robed statues, not portraits of the 78 real works.
- Pedestals, the 0.55 m parapets, canal walls, canal depth (bed 1 m below water), bridge arches, the ring balustrade texture (original artwork, `Assets/Art/Landmarks/Textures/RingBalustrade.png`) and the fountain bowl and jet are also typology.
- Statues face the canal.

**Gameplay.** An invisible 1.6 m barrier along the parapets and bridge balustrades keeps walkers out of the canal. A hidden bed stops anything that falls in.
